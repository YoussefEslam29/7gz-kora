7GZ-KORA

Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

7GZ-KORA is an app for friends who play football together.

What we keep

  • From your Google account when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • What you do in the app: the groups you join, your replies to nights, check-in times, teams, goals, votes, ratings, and whether you have paid your share of the pitch.
  • Your player card: shirt number, strong foot, position, motto, the five skills you rate yourself on, and whether you show those skills to others.
  • If you add one: your InstaPay number, so people can pay you their share of the pitch.
  • Chat messages you write in your groups (a deleted message's text is removed).
  • Group photos that admins upload.

Who can see it

Members of your groups see what happens in those groups, the chat included, and your InstaPay number if you added one. Anyone signed in to 7GZ-KORA can find you by name and see your photo, your card (your skills only if you show them) and your totals; they never see your email or phone number. A group photo can be seen by anyone who has its web address. Votes stay secret until voting closes; then the group sees who voted what. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for ads.

Where it is kept

The data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and group photos), and the app is hosted on Vercel.

Removing your data

You can leave a group at any time in the app (More → Leave this group). To have your account and data deleted completely, ask your group admin or write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
