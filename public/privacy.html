7GZ-KORA

Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

7GZ-KORA is a private app for a group of friends who play football together. You can only use it with an invite code from someone in the group.

What we keep

  • From your Google account when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • What you do in the app: the groups you join, your replies to nights, check-in times, teams, goals, votes, ratings, and whether you have paid your share of the pitch.

Who can see it

Only the members of your group. Votes stay secret until voting closes; then the group sees who voted what. Nothing is public, nothing is sold, and nothing is used for ads.

Where it is kept

The data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in), and the app is hosted on Vercel.

Removing your data

You can leave a group at any time in the app (More → Leave this group). To have your account and data deleted completely, ask your group admin or write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
